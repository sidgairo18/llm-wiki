# Research Overview

*This page is the evolving synthesis of everything in the wiki. It will be rewritten as sources accumulate.*

## Focus Areas

### Vision-Language Models
Architectures and training paradigms that jointly learn visual and linguistic representations. Key threads: contrastive pretraining (CLIP lineage), generative VLMs (LLaVA, Flamingo lineage), scaling laws, and emergent capabilities.

### Representation Learning
How to learn transferable visual features. Includes contrastive methods (SimCLR, MoCo, DINO), masked image modeling (MAE, BEiT), and the convergence toward foundation model representations (DINOv2, SigLIP).

### Generative Diffusion Models
The dominant paradigm for image and video synthesis. Key threads: denoising score matching → DDPM → latent diffusion → DiT/flow matching for images; SVD → Sora-class models for video. Connections to representation learning (diffusion features as representations) and VLMs (text-conditioned generation).

### Mechanistic Interpretability
Understanding what models learn internally. Probing classifiers, feature visualization, sparse autoencoders (SAEs), circuit-level analysis, causal interventions. Increasingly applied to vision models and multimodal systems.
